# Hook pattern catalogue

General mechanisms. Always prefer the niche-specific formulas in `research/patterns.md`. This list is the fallback
vocabulary for describing what an outlier is doing.

| Pattern | Mechanism | Example shape | Works when | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Result-first | Show the end state, then rewind | "This [result] cost me $40. Here's how." | Result is visual and surprising | Result is ordinary |
| Contrarian | Attack a belief the viewer holds | "Stop [common advice]. It's why your [X] is [bad]." | Niche has entrenched advice | You can't back it up within 30 s |
| Stat-shock | A number that breaks expectations | "[N]% of [group] get [X] wrong." | Number is real and specific | Number is vague ("most people") |
| Stakes / cost | What the viewer loses by not watching | "If you [do X], you're losing [concrete thing]." | Loss is concrete | Fear-mongering, vague loss |
| Challenge / experiment | Costly effort the viewer won't make | "I [did extreme thing] for [time]." | Effort is real and visible | Outcome is obvious |
| Question (specific) | Opens a loop the viewer can't close | "Why does [specific paradox] happen?" | Question is specific and odd | Generic ("Want to get better at X?") |
| Story cold-open | Drop into the middle of a moment | "Three hours before the deadline, the [thing] broke." | Strong narrator, real stakes | Slow setup |
| List promise | Bounded, skimmable value | "[N] [things] that [outcome], #[N] is [twist]." | Search-driven niches | Items are filler |
| Pattern break | Visual or audio surprise in frame 1 | Unexpected action/prop/location | Shorts, feed-scroll contexts | No payoff for the surprise |

## Anatomy of a strong first 5 seconds
1. **Frame 1 is already the video.** No logo, no "hey guys", no establishing shot.
2. **Curiosity gap + credibility.** Say what's surprising *and* why to believe it.
3. **Visual matches the claim.** If the hook says "$40 setup", the $40 setup is on screen.
4. **Payoff is promised, not delivered.** The hook opens the loop, and the video closes it later.

## Re-hook lines (mid-video)
- "But that's not the part that surprised me."
- "Which sounds fine, until you see [X]."
- "And this is where most people quit."
- "Remember [setup from hook]? Here's why it matters."

## Red flags in a draft hook
- Starts with "In this video…" / "Today I'm going to…" / a greeting
- Promise arrives after 15 s
- Two ideas competing in the hook
- A claim with no visual or proof attached
