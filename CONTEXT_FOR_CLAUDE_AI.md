## 3. Korean advertising rules to follow

| ✅ OK | ❌ Never |
|---|---|
| 피부 미백에 도움 / 피부 주름개선에 도움 | 식약처 인증, 식약처 허가 |
| 미백·주름개선 2중 기능성 화장품 | 재생, 치료, 염증 |
| 나이아신아마이드 2% · 아데노신 0.04% 함유 | 주름 제거 |
| 유산균 발효 PDRN 함유 (with footnote) | 무독성, 100% 안전 |
| 중금속·미생물 불검출 (*자사 시험성적서 기준) | 피부과/저자극 테스트 완료 |
| 파라벤·페녹시에탄올·알코올 무첨가 | 어려보이는, 동안 |
| 제조사 직접 생산, 전수 검수 | 완벽, 압도적 (superlatives) |
| | Fake celebrity endorsements |

- Put a source footnote under every claim, for example "*자사 시험성적서 기준" or "*개인차가 있을 수 있습니다".
- Recommend the Korea Cosmetic Association's advertising review (대한화장품협회 광고자문) before launch.

## 5. Positioning
- **One-liner:** "제조사가 직접 만든, 미백·주름개선 2중 기능성 발효 마스크"
- **USPs, in priority order:**
  1. Dual functional cosmetic (2% niacinamide + adenosine)
  2. **Made directly by the manufacturer** (mixing → filling → sealing → batch coding → hand inspection). No competitor shows this.
  3. COA: heavy metals and microbes not detected
  4. 7 fermented ingredients plus fermentation-derived PDRN (supporting)
  5. BTGin's own red-ginseng ginsenosides
  6. 4 types of hyaluronic acid plus 23ml of essence
  7. 텐셀 시트
  8. Free-from list
- **Main target:** women aged 25–39. Secondary targets are 40+, ingredient-conscious shoppers and gift buyers.
- **Competitor benchmark (Medicube PDRN Pink Collagen Gel Mask page):**
  - It persuades with more than 20 human-trial percentages, visual demos (color change, a weight test, a thermal camera), named ingredients ("Prism PDRN™", "310Da collagen") and footnotes on every claim.
  - It has the **same** 2중 기능성 claim, so that claim alone isn't a differentiator.
  - It never shows where or how its product is made, which is our edge.
  - Borrow the structure only. Never copy their images, wording or layout (their page carries a copyright warning).
