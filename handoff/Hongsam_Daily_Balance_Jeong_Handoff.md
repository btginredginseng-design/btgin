## 5. Marketing analysis

**Positioning:** *the smooth, easy daily red ginseng that also makes a proper gift.* Lead with feeling ("하루 한 포, 나를 챙기는 시간"), then support it with box facts: 6년근 국내산 홍삼, 14 ingredients, HACCP and in-house production.

**Competitive frame (Coupang / Naver):**
- **vs red ginseng sticks and 진액.** Those are strong, bitter and premium-priced. Ours is easier to drink: one pouch, softened with jujube, pear and honey.
- **vs cheap sweet ginseng or jujube drinks.** Those are often thin on real ginseng. Ours has 6-year Korean red ginseng, 14 ingredients and a gift-grade box.

**Brand essence:** the feeling is *being cared for*. In Korea red ginseng is how families say "I'm thinking of you", and Daily Balance turns that gesture into a small daily ritual.

## 7. Claims guardrails (critical)

As a general food, the page may not claim, or hint at, an effect on the body. That applies to text, images, tags, GIF captions and reposted reviews.

| 사용 가능 | 사용 불가 |
| --- | --- |
| 6년근 국내산 홍삼을 담았어요 (with proof) | 면역력 강화, 피로 회복, 혈행 개선, 기력 회복 |
| 대추·배·벌꿀로 부드러운 맛 | 숙취 해소, 간 건강 (despite 헛개) |
| 하루 한 포, 나를 챙기는 시간 | 몸의 균형을 맞춰줘요, 호르몬 균형 |
| 14가지 원료 K-Herbs Recipe | 해독, 디톡스, 노화 방지 |
| HACCP 인증 시설에서 생산 | 건강기능식품, 식약처 인정 |
| 공인 시험기관 검사 적합 (full report shown) | 의사 추천, 임상으로 입증 |
| 당류 3g (if label confirms) | 무설탕, 제로슈거 |
| 온 가족이 함께 즐기는 부드러운 맛 | 성장기 아이에게 좋아요, 임산부에게 좋아요 |

**Do not copy CK30's efficacy language.** It includes "약리효과 극대화", "흡수율 58배", "한국인 25%는 흡수 불가", "3개월 섭취 시 변화" and body/organ graphics, and those carry regulatory risk even for CK30 (also a general food, 고형차). Daily Balance should borrow CK30's **design quality and structure**, not its claims.

### 8.2 Narrative pattern (what makes it persuasive)

1. **Emotional or brand hook** (soil, roots, moss, dark luxury).
2. **Problem:** "good ingredients are useless if not absorbed", plus customer pain points.
3. **Education:** what saponin is and why absorption matters, told with animated science visuals.
4. **Solution:** the brand's process (효소홍삼 / ECS).
5. **Proof:** charts with cited sources, comparison table, patent count.
6. **Differentiation:** generation timeline ("5세대 홍삼"), golden ratio (7:3).
7. **Trust:** research team, own facility, patents and certificates.
8. **Gift and use cases**, then "이런 분들께 추천합니다".
9. **Required info:** nutrition, 상세정보, legal notices.

### 8.3 Design system

- **Colours:**
  - Deep green #06332C / #033327
  - Mid green #286345 / #3F6D63
  - Gold-beige highlight #F7F4B1
  - Gold gradient text
  - Beige paper #E2DDC9 / #E3D7C7
  - Warm off-white #F2EBE3
  - Grey #E6E7EC
  - Black #000 / #14140C for luxury sections
- **Type:**
  - Bold sans for headlines (Noto Sans KR / 본고딕 style), with key words in green or gold.
  - Light sans for lead-in lines above headlines ("알고 계신가요?", "그리고 무엇보다 중요한 사실").
  - Serif for the brand mark.
  - Vertical calligraphy for one emotional moment.
- **Layout:**
  - 900 px wide; sections alternate dark and light backgrounds.
  - Each section has a small lead-in line, then a 2-line headline with one coloured keyword, then 1–2 lines of body copy.
  - Numbered badges (01/02/03) and "~이 특별한 이유 첫번째/두번째/세번째" tags structure the argument.
- **Photography:** dark, moody tabletop styling (moss, driftwood, velvet, brass, glassware); hands in frame; macro texture shots.
- **GIF techniques used:**
  - Cinematic product reveal (light sweep).
  - Hands-in-action video loops.
  - Pouring powder or liquid.
  - Self-drawing charts.
  - Icon fill and "UP" pop.
  - Highlight cycling across cards.
  - Glowing x-ray bodies.
  - Beaker fill comparison.
  - Badge pop-in.
  - Lab B-roll.

| Reuse | Avoid / change |

**Visual identity shift:** CK30 is dark green, gold and luxury-clinical. Daily Balance packaging is **bright red, maroon and gold**, and the brand idea is warm daily care. Use **warm cream/off-white backgrounds, product red and maroon, gold accents and natural light**. Use dark maroon (not green) for the luxury or gift sections, so the two BTGin products are related but distinct.

## 10. Production specs for images and GIFs

- **Canvas:**
  - 860 px (Naver-safe) or 900 px wide.
  - Export slices at most ~1,500–2,000 px tall.
  - JPG for photos (quality 80–85, aim < 1 MB each); PNG only for flat graphics.
- **GIFs:**
  - Keep each under **~5 MB**. CK30's 17–29 MB GIFs are too heavy for mobile and risk upload limits.
  - How: 10–15 fps, 2–4 s loops, 128–256 colours, crop to the action area (e.g. 860×480), and avoid full-frame noisy footage.
  - Check current Naver and Cafe24 upload limits before export.
- **Text:**
  - Put Korean text into the design as live text in Figma/Photoshop, **not** generated by AI.
  - Fonts: Pretendard or Noto Sans KR for body and headlines; an optional serif (Noto Serif KR / 나눔명조) for one emotional line.
- **Accessibility and SEO:** also enter key text in the platform's text fields (Naver product attributes, Cafe24 summary). Image-only pages are not searchable.

### Gemini (image and video generation) guidance

- **Never let the AI redraw the packaging.** It will garble the 진홍삼 label.
  - Generate **text-free backgrounds and scenes**, then composite the **real product photos** (photos 1–5) on top with matching shadows.
  - Or use image editing with the real photo as input and ask it to keep the product unchanged.
- **Never ask the AI for Korean text** in images; add it in the design tool.
- Keep a consistent look: warm cream/off-white, product red, maroon and gold, soft natural window light, shallow depth of field.
- For GIFs:
  - Generate a short video clip (or use the production footage).
  - Trim to 2–4 s, then convert with ffmpeg (palettegen/paletteuse) at 860 px wide, 12 fps.
